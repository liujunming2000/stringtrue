ENGLISH

Overview

StringTrue (弦准) is a free, closed-source instrument tuner. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is (or is not) used.

Data We Collect

None. StringTrue does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • The microphone is used only for live pitch detection while the app is open.
  • No audio is recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • No pitch/frequency history is persisted on the device or elsewhere.
  • The app requires no user account and no network access.
  • The only Android permission requested is RECORD_AUDIO (microphone), used solely for tuning.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, because no data is collected in the first place. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no in-app purchases, and no donation feature; it involves no payment of any kind.

Children's Privacy

StringTrue does not target children, but because it collects no data, it is safe for users of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL and reflected in the in-app "Privacy Policy" section.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the app's store listing.

概述

弦准是一款免费闭源调音应用。本政策说明应用处理的数据及其使用方式。

我们收集的数据

不收集任何数据。 弦准不收集、存储或传输任何个人信息。

  • 麦克风 仅 在应用打开期间用于实时音高检测。
  • 不录制、不保存、不上传任何音频。
  • 不在设备或任何位置保存音高/频率历史。
  • 应用 不要求用户账户，也 不访问网络。
  • 申请的唯一个 Android 权限是 RECORD_AUDIO（麦克风），仅用于调音。

第三方共享

我们不向任何第三方共享数据，因为从根本上不收集任何数据。应用不含任何分析 SDK、广告 SDK、应用内购买或打赏功能，亦不涉及任何支付。

儿童隐私

弦准不针对儿童，但由于不收集数据，任何年龄的用户均可安全使用。
